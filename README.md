This repository documents my learning journey and independent work in
**Quantum Computing**, inspired in part by my participation in the
**Qiskit Global Summer School 2026 (QGSS26) — "A Decade on the Cloud"**,
organized by IBM Quantum.

As I began exploring quantum computing more systematically, QGSS26 provided
an opportunity to move beyond theoretical concepts and work hands-on with
quantum programming and quantum-computing workflows.

The program combined:

- In-depth quantum computing lectures
- Hands-on programming with Qiskit
- Quantum circuit construction and execution
- Quantum algorithms and simulation
- Real quantum hardware
- Noise, hardware limitations, and benchmarking
- Quantum advantage and quantum simulation
- Optimization
- Quantum + HPC hybrid workflows
- Interaction with researchers, engineers, and the global quantum community

This repository is intended to serve as a **technical record of my continued
learning, experimentation, implementations, and exploration of research
directions in quantum computing**.
